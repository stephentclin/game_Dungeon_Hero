# game_Dungeon_Hero

Dungeon Hero is a puzzle auto-battler where you play as the commander of a dungeon.

The screen is split into two parts. On the right side, you control a puzzle board and match icons to summon monsters. On the left side, the battlefield runs automatically, and your summoned monsters will fight the hero by themselves.

You do not directly control the units in battle. Your main job is to operate the puzzle board, create useful matches, and send enough monsters into the battlefield. Each match helps build your monster army and gives you more pressure against the hero.

Your goal is simple: protect the dungeon and completely defeat the hero before they break through.

[How to Play]
Use the right-side puzzle board to match icons.
Matching icons will summon monsters.
The left-side battlefield fights automatically.
Keep summoning monsters to stop the hero.
Defeat the hero before the dungeon falls.
